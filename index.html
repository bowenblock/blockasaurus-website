Ten mountains, ten mechanics

  • Bunny Slope — learn the ropes on gentle powder.
  • Timber Run — dodge logging trucks or crush them for coins.
  • Glacier Peak — slip across icy walkways and frozen lakes.
  • Serpent Pass — hug the inside line or fall off the cliff.
  • Avalanche Alley — outrun launching boulders.
  • Lunar Surface — float through low gravity and leap craters.
  • Stone Temple — smash through ancient pillar gates.
  • Flame Temple — dodge bursting fire traps.
  • Water Temple — ride bridges over deep lakes and crashing waves.
  • Sky Temple — survive sky drops off floating platforms.
